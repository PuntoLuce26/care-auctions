How a photo enters this app

  1. Gianluca shoots after the staging and saves the files in one folder.
  2. Sam verifies every image (Claude Code vision: content, order, duplicates).
  3. Files are converted, placed in assets/foto/, and the slots above are filled.
  4. The app is re-verified: offline, weight, and no unverified content.
