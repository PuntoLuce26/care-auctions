Cookie Policy

This app does NOT use cookies

This application uses no cookies, no trackers, no third-party services, no advertising and no profiling. It makes no network requests: everything runs on your device.

What we store, and where

The only data stored is kept on your device (browser localStorage) and is never sent anywhere:

  • Text size preference (the "Aa" control)
  • Your consent choices (the Permissions & privacy page)
  • Your saved analyses (the dashboard)

You can clear all of it at any time from your browser settings, or with the "Revoke all" button on the Permissions & privacy page.

When the online version arrives

When the app moves to a server (phase 2), this policy will be updated before publication, and any cookie or tracking introduced at that stage will be declared here — with the same principle: minimum data, explicit purpose, full transparency.
