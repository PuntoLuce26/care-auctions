The Manifesto.

The soul of iCARe.

There was once a family that dreamed of a home.
Not a palace. A home: a place where light enters in the morning, where children grow,
where the door closes and the world stays outside.

But the world had put a wall between the family and its home.
The price.

So iCARe was born.

An intelligence that reads thousands of auctions.
A human being who holds the hand.
Together, they do something never done before:
they take the wall down.

iCARe does not sell houses.
iCARe returns houses.

It does not promise discounts.
It promises safety — because when you buy from the tribunal,
the past of the house is erased.
The house is reborn. With you.

And while it does this, iCARe repopulates the villages,
brings life back where silence remained,
and with twenty percent of everything it earns,
it donates homes to the most fragile.

Because a house is not a luxury.
A house is the first right of every human being.

The creed.

A home for everyone.
Today, in Italy.
Tomorrow, in every corner of the world.

The words, forever.

  • "The auction will never be the same."
  • "Buy from the tribunal. The house is reborn with you."
  • "The intelligence works the numbers. The human holds the hand."
  • "A home for everyone."
  • "Zero fees, until you win. And when you win, twenty percent returns to humanity."

— la proprietà, the property of PuntoLuce and careauctions.co
