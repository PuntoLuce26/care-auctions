Why the tribunal is the safest purchase.

When you win an auction and pay the balance, the tribunal issues the decree of transfer: it erases the entire past of the property, on paper, everything. The property is reborn with you. The next deed is simple: the notary does not even need the twenty-year search — the decree is enough. What is not erased is written in the sale notice, our bible. The decree takes about 30-40 days; the deed is needed only in bankruptcy and dormant-inheritance cases.
